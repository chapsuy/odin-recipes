Grilled Porkchops

These grilled pork chops are simple to prepare with an easy marinade you can make in the morning for a tasty dinner that cooks on the grill in just 10 minutes.

What Makes These Pork Chops So Tender?

The secret to these pork chops is the marinade. A mix of olive oil, soy sauce, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, garlic, basil, parsley, and black pepper helps season the meat while keeping it juicy. A few hours in the marinade gives the pork time to soak up those flavors before grilling.

Can You Use Bone-In or Boneless Pork Chops?

This recipe calls for boneless pork chops, but you can use bone-in chops if you prefer. Bone-in chops tend to stay juicier, while boneless chops cook more quickly and evenly. For best results, choose chops that are at least 1 inch thick and cook them to an internal temperature of 145 degrees F (63 degrees C).

Ingredients:

  • ½ cup water
  • ⅓ cup light soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons lemon pepper seasoning
  • 3 tablespoons lemon pepper seasoning
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 6 boneless pork loin chops, trimmed of fat

Steps:

  1. Step 1

    Mix water, soy sauce, vegetable oil, lemon-pepper seasoning, and garlic in a deep bowl; add pork chops and toss to coat. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours.

  2. Step 2

    Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate.

  3. Step 3

    Remove pork chops from the marinade and shake off excess; discard the remaining marinade.

  4. Step 4

    Cook the pork chops on the preheated grill until no longer pink in the center, 5 to 6 minutes per side. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read 145 degrees F (63 degrees C).

  5. Step 5

    Serve hot and enjoy!
